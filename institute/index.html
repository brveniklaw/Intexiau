Our Belief · Economic Authorship

Every person has the inherent right to be the active author of their own economic — and civic — life.

Economic Authorship is the conviction beneath everything we do. We don't hand you answers or automate you out of the story. We make you the author — capable, rooted, and ready to build in an age that will reward those who can.

What We Are

One conviction, two expressions.

The Institute develops the whole author — the skill to build an economic life, and the roots of a civic one. Together they are what "human flourishing" actually means.

▲

The Institute

Learn, build, and earn real credentials. Hands-on programs where you gain genuine AI skills and launch actual ventures — guided every step, grounded in real problems, never busywork.

The Innovation Center

How We Teach

You learn by authoring, not by watching.

Three principles run through every program — the reason people finish able to use AI, not just talk about it.

01

Problem-First Learning

You start with a real challenge from your own life or work — not a lecture. Theory arrives only when you need it.

02

Real Work, Every Day

Each lesson ends with an exercise you actually complete and a short written reflection on what you did. What you make is yours to keep.

03

AI Practice in Every Lesson

Every day includes a ready-to-use prompt and clear guidance on checking what AI tells you — so you build skill and judgment together.
